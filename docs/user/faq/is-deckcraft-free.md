Yes. DeckCraft is free and open source. The code is dual-licensed under MIT or Apache-2.0, at your option. The app page on getartcraft.com says it has no subscription and no cloud.

The ArtCraft name, wordmark and logos are trademarks of the ArtCraft Team and are not covered by that license. Fonts, icons and other assets keep their own open licenses, listed in `ATTRIBUTION.md` in the repository.

Sources: the License section of the [README](https://github.com/storytold/deckcraft#license-and-credits).
