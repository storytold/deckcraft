Select a slide, open the **Transitions** tab and click an effect in the gallery. Click **Preview** to see it.

## Steps

1. Select the slide in the slide list on the left.
2. Open the **Transitions** tab.
3. Click an effect in the gallery. The small button at the right of the gallery opens the full list, grouped as **Subtle**, **Exciting** and **Dynamic Content**.
4. If the effect has variants, click **Effect Options** and choose one, such as **From Left** or **Horizontal**.
5. Set **Duration:** in seconds.
6. Choose how the show moves on: tick **On Mouse Click**, or tick **After:** and set the seconds to wait.
7. Click **Preview** to play the transition.

To give every slide the same transition, click **Apply To All**.

## Check the duration of imported transitions

In DeckCraft 0.7.0 and earlier, a transition imported from a PowerPoint file can show the wrong **Duration:**. See [Imported transitions are too slow](../troubleshooting/imported-transitions-too-slow.md).
