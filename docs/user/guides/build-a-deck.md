Pick a theme on the start screen, add slides from the **Home** tab with **New Slide**, and change the look any time from the **Design** tab.

## Start a deck

1. Open the start screen. If a deck is already open, click the **Home** icon at the left of the title bar. The back arrow at the top left returns you to your deck, and so does Esc.
2. Under **New**, click **Blank Presentation** or a theme tile. **More themes** lists every theme.

## Add slides

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. Click **New Slide** to add a slide. Or click the arrow next to it and pick a layout from the gallery.
3. To change the layout of an existing slide, click **Layout** and pick one.
4. Click **Reset** to put a slide's placeholders back to their layout positions and formatting.

The arrow menu next to **New Slide** also has **Duplicate Selected Slides** and **Slides from Outline…**.

## Group slides in sections

1. Click **Section** on the **Home** tab.
2. Choose **Add Section**. **Remove All Sections** takes them all away again.

## Change the theme later

1. Open the **Design** tab.
2. Click a theme in the gallery.
3. Use **Colors** and **Fonts** to swap the color scheme or the font pair, and **Background Styles** for the slide background.

## Add content

The **Insert** tab has **Table**, **Pictures**, **Shapes**, **Icons**, **SmartArt**, **Chart**, **Link**, **Comment**, **Text Box**, **Header & Footer**, **WordArt**, **Date & Time**, **Slide Number**, **Equation**, **Symbol**, **Video** and **Audio**.
