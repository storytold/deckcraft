Press F5 to play from the first slide. Press Esc to stop.

## Start the show

1. Open the **Slide Show** tab.
2. Click **Play from Start** or **Play from Current Slide**.

The title bar has the same two ways in: the **Play from Start (F5)** icon and the **Present** button. **Present** starts from the selected slide.

## Presenter view

The **Presenter View** button on the **Slide Show** tab is on by default. While it is on, the show opens a second window titled "Presenter View — DeckCraft" with the slide number, the next slide, your notes ("No notes." when a slide has none), and **End Show** and **Next ▶** buttons. Click **Presenter View** again to turn it off.

## Keys during the show

| Key                                                 | What it does            |
|-----------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Right arrow, Down arrow, Space, Page Down, N, Enter | Next step or slide      |
| Left arrow, Up arrow, Backspace, Page Up, P         | Previous step or slide  |
| A number, then Enter                                | Go to that slide        |
| Home, End                                           | First slide, last slide |
| B or . (period)                                     | Black screen on or off  |
| W or , (comma)                                      | White screen on or off  |
| Ctrl+P                                              | Pen on or off           |
| Ctrl+A                                              | Pen off                 |
| E                                                   | Erase pen marks         |
| Esc or -                                            | End the show            |

On macOS, Ctrl+P and Ctrl+A become Cmd+P and Cmd+A.

Clicking the slide moves on to the next step, and a right click goes back.

## Hide a slide

Select the slide and click **Hide Slide** on the **Slide Show** tab. **Set Up Slide Show** has the show options, and **Rehearse Timings** plays the show and records how long you stay on each slide.
