Screen recording: type every media request from its source name, report an empty take as empty, and let an album edit reopen itself (#1815)

- picture.change, shape.fill, slide.background, smartart and transition.set type their bytes with
  detect_name like insert.picture, so a path's own extension decides even when the caller sent a
  display name without one.
- An empty take (Stop before the first frame, say with the portal picker open) reports "had nothing
  in it" instead of "couldn't be written: nothing was recorded": RecorderError::Empty is not a write
  failure.
- The picker says "select a screen when recording" for a host that picks its own source, and keeps
  "no screen found" for one with no available source.
- The album test reopens the album through insert.photoAlbumEdit, checks the seed carries typeName
  and re-inserts the seeded items.
